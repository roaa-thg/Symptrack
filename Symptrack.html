How we built it

We mapped 24 disease labels into three triage categories using a CTAS-based protocol, fine-tuned ClinicalBERT with a weighted loss to handle class imbalance, and added a confidence threshold that defaults uncertain cases to the safest category. We compared ClinicalBERT against three GRU models to confirm that contextual, medically pre-trained language understanding was the key to reliable triage.

Disclaimer: Symptrack is an academic research prototype. It provides triage guidance only and is not a medical diagnosis. Always consult a healthcare professional. In an emergency, call 997.
